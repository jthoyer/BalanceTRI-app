Buttons

All interactive controls keep a 44px minimum height/width for touch targets. Buttons are borderless with color and weight doing the differentiation.

Primary & secondary

Primary is the single highest-emphasis action per screen (e.g. add / save / create). Secondary is for the paired dismiss action. In the header, order runs Refresh → Add a race → account cluster (name, Admin, Sign out), with Sign out at the far edge. 

<button class="primary-button">Add a race <span>+</span></button>
<button class="secondary-button">Cancel</button>
<button class="text-button">Refresh</button>
<button class="icon-button" aria-label="Close">×</button>

Save / utility buttons

The roster's per-entry .roster-edit button opens that entry in the commitment form below it — it never deletes anything itself, so its hover is neutral. Removing is a separate, explicit action inside the form: .remove-commit-button, which only appears while editing an existing entry, carries the destructive red hover. Adding a first commitment starts collapsed behind .commit-toggle-button rather than showing the form open by default. 

<button class="save-button">Save commitment</button>
<button class="commit-toggle-button">Add your commitment</button>      <!-- collapses the form until clicked -->
<button class="roster-edit">Edit</button>                             <!-- per roster entry; neutral hover -->
<button class="remove-commit-button">Remove commitment</button>       <!-- inside the form only; hover: red -->

Choice pills

Used for event/distance selection and commitment level. Selected state has a neutral navy style by default; the locked level gets its own green treatment so a firm commitment reads as unmistakably "done."

Commitment levels

There is no "not going" option — someone who isn't racing simply has no entry, so the roster only ever lists people who are actually in.

Form fields

Inputs share one style across the app: 1px border, 9px radius, 44px min height, white fill — including "Race name" in the add-race form, which matches "View an athlete's races" exactly rather than falling back to browser defaults.

Add/Edit race — Balance Bolt field set

Selecting Balance Bolt in Event type reduces the add-race and edit-race forms to Event type, Race # and Date. Race website, Events or distances and the Club focus race checkbox are hidden with .hidden, and the .field-name label swaps to "Race #" with an e.g. 3 placeholder. Any other type — including the blank "Select type…" — restores all six fields and the "Race name" label. Driven by applyEventTypeFields(form) in app.js, shared by #raceForm and #editForm; the hidden fields keep their values rather than being cleared, and the checkbox is not needed because every Balance Bolt race is saved with club_focus true.

Components

Composite pieces built from the tokens and primitives above.

Race list card

.race-summary uses --brand-blue as its own background (.club-focus only — a non-club-focus race stays white), independent of the ink/navy used for the page header.

It is an <a>, not a <button>. Every race has its own shareable URL — <base>race/<slug>, e.g. /BalanceTRI-app/race/berlin-marathon-2026 — so the card is real navigation and carries a real href. That is what makes "open in new tab" and "copy link address" work, and it also makes the markup valid (an <h2> is not allowed inside a <button>). app.js intercepts only the plain left click and calls preventDefault(); modified clicks are handed back to the browser. The only CSS the element change needs is text-decoration:none — colour, layout and focus ring are already element-agnostic.

There's no inline-expanding state: a click navigates to a full #raceScreen (below), not an in-place expansion of the card.

Race screen — race details

Opening a race hides the list and toolbar and shows #raceScreen in their place — a dedicated screen with its own back link, not an inline expansion of the card above. Its body (.details-card) uses grey .card-header labels as the only separators, full width and height, no nested panel. Roster entries are grouped by distance (alpha), and names within each group are alpha-sorted too. Each distance label (.roster-group-label) uses --brand-blue at the same 12px size as .card-header. Commitment level is kept separate from distance: it shows as a small pill (.roster-level) next to each name. Clicking a roster entry's .roster-edit button (never a delete — see Buttons, above) opens it in the commitment form below; that form starts collapsed behind .commit-toggle-button for a member with no entry yet. In "Your commitment," each group — commitment level, participation type, the add-a-distance field — gets its own .choice-label: the same bold/uppercase/12px treatment as .card-header, but in --brand-blue rather than grey, so it reads as a sub-heading within the section instead of another top-level header.

View toggle

.view-toggle sits in the hero, over the dark ink background, so its unselected state is a translucent white rather than the light-surface tones used everywhere else. The selected segment always shows — it's a toggle between "Club races" and "View all," never a state where nothing is selected.

</div>

Event-type & club-focus badges

Both sit inline after the race title in .race-info h2, on the race card and the race screen alike. .event-type-badge (navy) names the discipline — Run, Bike, Swim, Multi-sport, Triathlon, or Balance Bolt; it's omitted when a race has no event type set. .club-focus-badge (yellow) marks a race the committee has flagged as a priority club race — the same flag that puts it under "Club races" in the view toggle above and gives its card the brand-blue background.

Race embed

Balance Bolt races replace the usual "Club commitments"/"Your commitment" sections entirely with an embedded iframe of the BalanceBolt sign-up page (.race-embed, under a "Race details" .card-header) — there's no roster or commitment form for these, since sign-up happens on BalanceBolt itself. The iframe resizes to its own content height via a same-origin ResizeObserver, and hides BalanceBolt's own topbar chrome once loaded, rather than showing a fixed-height scrollbar-in-a-scrollbar. Not shown live here — the specimen below is a static stand-in — because embedding an iframe of another origin's page inside this guide would need that page's own cooperation.

Feedback & states

Focus ring

Applied via :focus-visible to buttons, links, and inputs — tab to the field below to see it.

Destructive hover

Update banner

.update-banner is a persistent success banner — green, with a checkmark — shown above the race list after a save. It isn't a toast: it doesn't time out or dismiss itself, and stays until the next action re-renders the page. Text comes from showToast(msg) in app.js, which despite the function name drives this banner, not a floating toast.

Commitment saved.

Auth sheet

The one overlay behind every sign-in entry point — the header button, the automatic signed-out nudge, and the write gate (attempting to save, edit, or remove without being signed in) all open this same role="dialog" aria-modal="true" sheet, never three different ones. A bottom sheet on desktop, full screen on mobile (styles-additions.css). As a "nudge" it's self-dismissing — tapping outside, the × , or "View Calendar" all close it the same way. As a "gate" the × just backs out of that specific save attempt. Either way it traps Tab within its own focusable elements, closes on Escape, and returns focus to whatever opened it.

Reduced motion

A global @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) rule disables scroll-behavior, transitions, and animation site-wide — no per-component opt-out needed.

Radius & spacing

Every rectangular element in the app shares one corner radius. Pills are the one deliberate exception — they stay fully round (999px) as a distinct shape, not a point on the radius scale.

Radius Preview Used on
9px Everything rectangular: .hero, .race-card, .add-panel, dialog, .empty-state, .primary-button/.secondary-button, .remove-commit-button, .save-button, text inputs
999px (pill) .choice, .view-toggle-btn, badges

Touch targets

Every interactive element (.text-button, .icon-button, .primary-button, .secondary-button, inputs, .choice, .save-button) enforces min-height: 44px for accessible tap sizing — with one deliberate exception: the roster's per-entry .roster-edit button is 32px, sized to sit quietly next to a name rather than dominate the row.

Responsive rules

Two breakpoints control layout collapse, resize this window (or the browser) to see them apply live to the page itself.
